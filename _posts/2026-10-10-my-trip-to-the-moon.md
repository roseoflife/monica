One night in Montreal, I looked out my window and saw the moon looking extra big and
extra bright. I made a wish, and suddenly my bed turned into a rocket ship!

We zoomed past the clouds, past the stars, and landed right on the moon. It was bouncy,
like a giant trampoline, so of course I did cartwheels and backflips all over the
moon's surface. I even drew a giant picture of my dog in the moon dust.

Then I waved goodbye to the stars and flew back home, just in time for pasta for
dinner.
