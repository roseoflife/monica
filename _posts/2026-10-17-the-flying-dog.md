Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to run and jump more than
any other dog in the whole city. One day, while doing a big jump off a trampoline, Max
jumped so high that he just... kept going!

Max flew over the rooftops, over the park, and over the ice cream shop (he grabbed a
scoop on the way, obviously). Everyone down below looked up and cheered.

When Max finally landed, he did a perfect gymnastics pose - arms up, big smile - and
everyone clapped. From that day on, Max was known as the Flying Gymnastics Dog of the
whole neighborhood.

**The End.**
